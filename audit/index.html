Free audit of your website or systems

Tell me what you want reviewed. I send back written feedback at no cost. If you then want me to fix or implement something, I send a fixed quote for that specific situation, and you decide.

Scope: I only review sites and systems that you own, or where the owner has given you written permission. I do not look at other people's systems without permission.

What the audit can cover

  • Website security: HTTPS, security headers, access and MFA, updates.
  • Backups: do they exist, are they off the server, has a restore been tested.
  • Technical SEO: indexing, sitemap, metadata, speed basics.
  • Automation: repetitive tasks that could be handled automatically.
  • Safe use of AI tools with business or personal data.
  • Business email: SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  • Team: phishing and scam awareness, accounts and permissions.

How it works

  1. You request it. Fill in the form below with what you want reviewed.
  2. You receive written feedback. Findings and recommendations, by email. The audit is free.
  3. If you want, you get a fixed quote. It covers the work for your situation. You decide whether to go ahead. There is no obligation to hire me.
