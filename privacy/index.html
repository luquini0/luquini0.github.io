Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who we are

Luquini0 is an independent IT services and security content project. This policy covers the website luquini0.github.io and the automation that answers comments and direct messages on the Instagram account @luquini0.

What the Instagram automation does

When you comment a keyword (for example AUDIT or BACKUP) on a @luquini0 post, or message the account, the automation replies publicly and/or sends you a direct message with a link to a free guide. It is used only to deliver the resource you asked for.

Data we process

  • Your Instagram username and numeric Instagram ID.
  • The text of your comment or message, and the ID of the post it was made on.
  • The status of our reply (sent or failed) and the date.

We do not collect passwords, payment data or any information outside Instagram's API for this purpose. We do not sell or share this data with third parties for advertising. It is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure used to run the automation, and Meta processes it under its own policies.

Free audit requests

If you submit the form on the free audit page, we store the name, email, site or system to review, topic and message you provide, plus a partial hash of your IP address used only to limit abuse (not the IP itself). We use them only to answer your request and, if you want one, to send you a quote. We do not sell or share them for advertising. They are kept on Cloudflare infrastructure and deleted on request (see below).

How long we keep it

We keep this data only as long as needed to avoid sending you duplicate messages and to follow up on your request, and delete it on request (see below).

Website

This site uses Google Analytics to measure aggregate traffic. It does not require an account and does not collect your name or email unless you write to us.

Data deletion instructions

To delete the data associated with your Instagram account, send an email to luquini0.contact@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram username, or send a direct message with those words to @luquini0 on Instagram. We will delete the records tied to your account and confirm by reply.
